OFFICIAL GOVERNMENT OPTIONS

Education Loans

Real education-loan schemes and the official portals students can use to apply or verify eligibility.

▤

PM-Vidyalaxmi Education Loan

Unified Government of India portal for education-loan applications and interest-subvention services.

  • Who: Students pursuing higher education in eligible Quality Higher Education Institutions (QHEIs).
  • Loan: Collateral-free and guarantor-free education loan under the PM-Vidyalaxmi scheme, subject to the scheme and bank conditions.
  • Income: Students of all family-income groups can seek the education loan; interest-subvention eligibility is separate.
  • Use: Tuition/course fees and eligible education-related expenses such as hostel, mess, laptop and living expenses as applicable.
Apply / Check PM-Vidyalaxmi →
%

PM-USP Central Sector Interest Subsidy (CSIS)

Interest support for eligible students who take an education loan for approved technical/professional courses.

  • Family income: Up to ₹4.5 lakh per year.
  • Course: Eligible technical/professional higher-education courses in approved institutions.
  • Benefit: Full interest subsidy during the moratorium period for eligible loans, subject to current CSIS rules.
  • Important: Eligibility depends on the institution, course, loan and scheme conditions.
Apply CSIS →

How to apply

1Register on PM-Vidyalaxmi
2Fill the Common Education Loan Application Form
3Select eligible banks and submit
4Track sanction, disbursement and applicable interest support

FinMate AI does not approve loans. Final sanction, interest rate, security requirements and disbursement are decided by the selected bank after verification.
