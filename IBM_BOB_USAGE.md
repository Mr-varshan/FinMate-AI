# IBM Bob Usage in FinMate AI

## Project Name

**FinMate AI – Your Smart Financial Companion for Student Life**

---

## 1. Overview

FinMate AI is a student-focused financial literacy and money management web application.

Many students begin managing their own money during college but may not have sufficient knowledge or practical tools to manage their finances effectively. Students often face challenges in tracking expenses, maintaining a budget, understanding loans, identifying scholarships, and making informed financial decisions.

FinMate AI addresses these challenges by providing a simple web platform that combines financial management features with AI-assisted development and intelligent financial guidance.

The project includes features such as:

- User registration and login
- Secure password handling
- Expense tracking
- Budget management
- Spending analysis
- Expense history
- Financial guidance
- Loan awareness
- Scholarship guidance
- Student-friendly user interface

IBM Bob was used as an AI-assisted development tool during the development workflow of the FinMate AI prototype.
